# cheat

Typing ZIPPY toggles cheat/debug mode. It remains enabled until reset or until you type ZIPPY again. Lowercase letters also work.


```
   Command after ZIPPY    Effect
  ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━  ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
   J                      Teleport helicopter toward the left side, coordinate $0260
  ─────────────────────  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   K                      Teleport helicopter to the middle, coordinate $07F7
  ─────────────────────  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   L                      Teleport helicopter toward the right side, coordinate $0DA0
  ─────────────────────  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   /                      Add one reserve helicopter/life
  ─────────────────────  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   -                      Toggle player-helicopter invulnerability
  ─────────────────────  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Return, then 1–8       Jump to the corresponding battlefield
  ─────────────────────  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Return, then 9         Skip toward the final briefing/ending sequence
  ─────────────────────  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Ctrl-A                 Toggle an internal hexadecimal debug/status display
```


Thus the familiar combined forms are ZIPPYJ, ZIPPYK, ZIPPYL, ZIPPY/, and ZIPPY-. For level selection, type ZIPPY,
press Return, then press the digit.


A couple of implementation details:


- Invulnerability prevents damage to the human player’s helicopter; it does not affect enemy units.
- / increments an 8-bit helicopter counter without enforcing a maximum.
- Ctrl-A may appear to do nothing in the retail game because the debug row is displayed only when internal diagnostic bytes have been queued.
- Ordinary digit keys 1–9 without first pressing Return merely change an internal HUD-animation cadence; they do not select a battlefield.
